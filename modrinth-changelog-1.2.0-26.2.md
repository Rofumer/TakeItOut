## 1.2.0

### Added

- **Refill Fireworks** (on by default, needs Auto Take Out). When you use the last firework rocket in your main hand or off hand, TakeItOut refills that hand from your inventory, a shulker box in your inventory, or a linked container. This works while flying with an elytra too, where Tweakeroo hand restock cannot help: vanilla removes the rocket only on the server, after Tweakeroo has already checked your hand.
  - Off-hand refills arrive in the main hand first and are moved to the off hand automatically; the item you were holding goes back into your main hand.
- **Litematica-Printer-Hana support.** Hana has its own TakeItOut integration: with Auto Take Out enabled, the printer requests missing materials from shulkers and linked containers by itself.

### Fixed

- The game no longer crashes on startup when Litematica-Printer-Hana is installed.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 26.2
